SMART MANGROVE EXPLORER

Mangrove Park, Panabo City, Davao del Norte

Tourist Journey Stage: EXPLORE

Enhancing the visitor experience through integrated smart technologies

01 · Tourism Problem

What Needs to Be Improved?

Mangrove Park in Panabo City has strong potential to offer an educational and enjoyable nature experience. However, two key issues currently limit its effectiveness:

  • Unengaging visitor experience — Limited interactive ways for tourists to learn about the mangrove ecosystem, wildlife, and conservation practices.
  • Inefficient data collection — Traditional paper logbooks are slow, often incomplete, and make it difficult for managers to organize, analyze, and act on visitor data.
02 · Smart Solution

Three Integrated Technologies

We propose a connected system that digitizes registration, provides intelligent on-site guidance, and captures real-time feedback — all working together to transform the EXPLORE stage.

🖥️ Digital Tourist Registration Kiosk

Touchscreen self-service at the entrance that replaces paper logbooks with structured digital data.

🤖 AI-Powered Digital Tourist Guide

Interactive chatbot / digital assistant that answers questions and educates visitors in real time.

📱 Smart Visitor Feedback & Monitoring System

QR-based digital feedback after the visit, feeding ratings and comments directly into a management dashboard.

Technology 1

Digital Tourist Registration Kiosk

A touchscreen computer or tablet placed at the park entrance. Visitors register digitally before entering — no more handwritten logbooks.

Data Collected

  • Name of visitor / group leader
  • Number of visitors in the party
  • Date and exact time of visit
  • Contact information (optional / for updates)
  • Purpose of visit (education, leisure, research, etc.)

Result: Faster entry, complete and searchable records, and immediate data available for tourism managers.

Technology 2

AI-Powered Digital Tourist Guide

A digital assistant or chatbot (accessible via kiosk, mobile web, or on-site tablets) that helps tourists explore and learn about Mangrove Park.

What visitors can ask

  • Mangrove species identification & facts
  • History of the park
  • Wildlife & biodiversity highlights
  • Conservation practices

Practical assistance

  • Activities and recommended spots
  • Safety rules and reminders
  • Directions to facilities
  • Trail tips and best times
Technology 3

Smart Visitor Feedback & Monitoring System

After exploring the park, visitors scan a QR code (or use a link on the kiosk) and submit feedback digitally in under a minute.

Visitors can rate & comment on

  • Cleanliness of trails and facilities
  • Condition and availability of amenities
  • Perceived safety
  • Quality of information provided (signage + digital guide)
  • Overall experience

Managers receive real-time insights instead of delayed or incomplete paper forms, enabling faster response and continuous improvement.

03 · Demonstration

How the Solution Works

1

Arrive & Register

Visitor uses the Digital Kiosk at the entrance to enter details in under 60 seconds.

2

Explore with AI

During the visit, the AI Digital Guide answers questions about species, trails, safety, and more.

3

Give Feedback

At exit (or via phone), scan QR code and rate cleanliness, facilities, safety, and overall experience.

4

Managers Act

Dashboard aggregates registrations and feedback for better planning, staffing, and conservation decisions.

04 · Smart Impact

How It Improves the Destination

🎯 Visitor Experience

Interactive, personalized learning makes the mangrove ecosystem more engaging and memorable.

⚡ Efficiency

Digital registration and feedback eliminate paper bottlenecks and speed up data handling.

♿ Accessibility

Multilingual AI guide and clear digital info support diverse visitors, including students and groups.

🌱 Sustainability

Better data helps manage carrying capacity and reinforces conservation messaging on-site.

🛡️ Safety

Instant reminders of rules, trail conditions, and emergency info reduce risks during exploration.

📊 Destination Mgmt

Real-time visitor insights enable evidence-based decisions for facilities, staffing, and promotion.
